# 📁 Sample Datasets for Decision Tree Studio

These sample datasets can be tested immediately in the **Upload Custom Dataset** mode.

---

### 1. `titanic.csv` (Titanic Survival)
- **File**: `sample_datasets/titanic.csv`
- **Rows**: 891 | **Columns**: 8
- **Target Column**: `Survived` (`0 = No`, `1 = Yes`)
- **Features**: `Pclass`, `Sex`, `Age`, `SibSp`, `Parch`, `Fare`, `Embarked`
- **Highlights**: Demonstrates automated handling of mixed types (categorical + continuous) and missing values.

---

### 2. `diabetes.csv` (Pima Indians Diabetes)
- **File**: `sample_datasets/diabetes.csv`
- **Rows**: 768 | **Columns**: 9
- **Target Column**: `Outcome` (`0 = Negative`, `1 = Positive`)
- **Features**: `Pregnancies`, `Glucose`, `BloodPressure`, `SkinThickness`, `Insulin`, `BMI`, `DiabetesPedigree`, `Age`
- **Highlights**: Standard clinical benchmark for metabolic disorder risk prediction.

---

### 3. `iris.csv` (Fisher's Iris Flower Classification)
- **File**: `sample_datasets/iris.csv`
- **Rows**: 150 | **Columns**: 5
- **Target Column**: `species` (`setosa`, `versicolor`, `virginica`)
- **Features**: `sepal_length`, `sepal_width`, `petal_length`, `petal_width`
- **Highlights**: Classic multi-class classification benchmark with clear visual decision boundaries.
